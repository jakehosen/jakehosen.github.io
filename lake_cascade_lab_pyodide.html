Lake Cascade Lab

A four-level food-chain model for the trophic cascade scenarios in Emery et al. (2016). Change a driver and see how piscivores, planktivores, zooplankton and phytoplankton respond over time, compared with an untouched control lake. The model runs in Python inside your browser.

Starting Python…
Based on Emery KA, Gephart JA, Wilkinson GM, Besterman AF, Pace ML (2016) Exploring trophic cascades in lake food webs with a spreadsheet model. In: Byrne L (ed) Learner-Centered Teaching Activities for Environmental and Sustainability Studies. Springer. DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-28543-6_13. Biomass units follow the spreadsheet. The equations are solved with scipy's LSODA solver in Python, running in the browser through Pyodide; output is daily. Needs an internet connection on first load.
